Section II
Principles

The Pillars of
Editorial Design

Editorial Precision

Every pixel placed with the care of a typesetter arranging lead blocks. Nothing accidental, everything intentional.

Typographic Hierarchy

Massive serif headlines command attention while body text delivers clarity. Information flows with natural rhythm.

Grid-Based Structure

Visible borders celebrate structure rather than hiding it. Columns create rhythm and guide the eye naturally.

Fig. 1.1— The Newsprint Grid System

Snappy Interactions

Fast, mechanical transitions. No bounce, no float. Elements snap into place with hard shadows and sharp edges.

Information Density

High density layouts honor the reader's time. Tight padding and efficient space usage deliver maximum content.

Timeless Authority

The aesthetic of record. Serious, trustworthy, and confident—like holding a fresh morning newspaper.

Section III
Methodology

How It Works

01

Set the Type

Choose your typographic scale with massive headlines and legible body text. Establish hierarchy through size and weight contrast.

02

Draw the Grid

Lay out your columns with explicit borders. Celebrate structure. Let visible grid lines create visual rhythm and order.

03

Ink the Page

Apply high-contrast black on off-white. Use editorial red sparingly. Add textures for depth. Ship with authority.
